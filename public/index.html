Questions

What Texans ask us first.

Still curious? The roof scan is free and comes with a 20-minute call with the engineer who drew your design.

What happens when the grid goes down?

The Vault senses the outage and separates your home from the grid in under 20 milliseconds, so lights, Wi-Fi and the fridge stay on. Your panels keep recharging the Vault during the day. With two Vaults, most homes run their essentials for three days or more.

How long from first call to a working system?

About five weeks for most homes. The install itself takes one or two days. The rest is design, city permits and utility approval, and we handle all of it.

Will the panels survive Texas hail?

S-440 panels use 3.2 mm tempered glass and are tested against 55 mm (2.2 inch) hail at 30 meters per second. Hail damage is covered by our 25-year warranty, parts and labor.

Do panels lose power in 100°F heat?

All panels make a little less power when they're hot. The S-440 loses 0.29% per °C above 77°F, roughly half the loss of older panels, and our mounts leave an air gap so the panels run cooler.

Can my HOA stop me from going solar?

In most cases, no. Texas Property Code §202.010 limits an HOA's power to ban rooftop solar, though it can set reasonable placement rules. We prepare and submit the HOA application for you.

Can I sell extra power back?

Yes, in most of Texas. In deregulated areas you choose a retail plan that pays for solar buyback. Austin Energy and CPS Energy run their own programs. We'll show you the best option for your ZIP code during design.
